# RSNC website refresh plan

**Specialized AI workers for B2B teams.** Resonance builds workers for roles a company would otherwise hire for, grounding each in the customer's recurring tasks, knowledge, rules, and tools through a Company Brain. Target operations and functional leads who own recurring work. Give IT and security reviewers direct access to integration, access-control, and handover details.

## The first screen

> **Automate the work round the clock.**
>
> We build specialized AI workers for your business and a Company Brain that gives them the knowledge, rules, and tools they need.
>
> **Hire AI Workers** · See clients & projects

Use a warm, full-width hero canvas with the RSNC wordmark, ink, and teal reserved for actions and workflow states. Spread a softly fading mosaic across the full hero behind the copy; blur and fade its left side under a light paper scrim to keep the headline, offer, and CTA crisp. Show 16 recognizable mid-market roles, including operations, customer support, inbound and outbound sales, finance, accounts payable, HR, recruiting, IT, procurement, customer success, marketing, social media, compliance, data analysis, and internal knowledge. Give each role one concise recurring task. On mobile, keep copy and CTA first, then show a compact two-column role mosaic. Use **Clients**, **AI workers**, and **Services** in navigation.

## Conversion

**Hire AI Workers → Contact page → Project brief → Qualified review → Discovery → Paid work**

Use the current Youform through **Open the project brief** on `/contact/`. Ask buyers to describe one recurring task and the tools or rules it depends on. Confirm a monitored email fallback, receipt, and reply owner. Review fit using frequency, ownership, system access, and a measurable bottleneck. Keep contact details and brief text out of analytics. [Current contact page](https://rsnc.tech/contact/) · [Youform submission tracking](https://help.youform.com/how-to-use-webhooks-in-youform-ub3hw)

**Primary KPI** is qualified reviews per 100 eligible visits. Use form receipts and CRM status to measure qualified pipeline and wins. Baseline 28 days where data exists. In five buyer sessions, look for at least four participants to identify a relevant worker, explain what the Company Brain supplies, and understand that **Hire AI Workers** starts a custom project inquiry. Review results at 30, 60, and 90 days.

## Answer-engine visibility

**Answer → Worked example → First-hand proof → Next step.** Use that pattern on existing service and use-case pages, with more text where buyers need it. Prioritize three questions.

- Can you automate work across our CRM and ERP?
- When should we use rules, an AI agent, or human review?
- How do we measure the return on one workflow?

Keep stable case URLs and factual references to systems, approval rules, and measured results. Align homepage metadata and visible copy around custom AI workers and the Company Brain. Serve text and links in initial HTML. Check canonicals, meaningful sitemap dates, Organization and breadcrumb markup, real 404s, and deployed crawler access. Keep OAI-SearchBot access separate from GPTBot training policy. Google Search ignores `llms.txt` and retired FAQ rich results. ## Launch gates

| Check | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Cold mobile | ≤200 KiB initial transfer, zero executable startup JavaScript, zero third-party startup requests |
| Mobile lab | Median of three fixed-profile runs. LCP ≤2.0 s, CLS ≤0.05, TBT ≤100 ms |
| Field p75 | LCP ≤2.5 s, INP ≤200 ms, CLS ≤0.10 as sample size permits |
| Responsive and accessible | WCAG 2.2 AA target. Check 320–1440 px, landscape, zoom, keyboard, screen reader, and reduced motion |
| Resilient | Reading, navigation, and contact link work with JS off. Verify form errors, receipt, CRM delivery, and email fallback |
| Discovery | Verify live status codes, raw HTML, crawler access, sitemap, and indexing signals for key pages |

Keep the static generator and GitHub Pages. The refresh serves one HTML implementation, uses optimized WebP assets, and opens the form on intent. The previous React entry and WebGL hero built about 323 kB of gzip JavaScript. The refresh targets zero executable startup JavaScript. Live transfer and field performance still need baselines. ## Release sequence

**Measure baseline → Test the hero and inquiry → Build and verify → Release and measure.** Preserve existing URLs. Keep the previous passing deploy ready if a key route or intake fails. Use buyer demand and approved evidence to choose the next case and workflow pages.
