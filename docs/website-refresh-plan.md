# RSNC website refresh plan

**Specialized AI workers for B2B teams.** Resonance builds workers for roles a company would otherwise hire for, grounding each in the customer's recurring tasks, knowledge, rules, and tools through a Company Brain. Target operations and functional leads who own recurring work. Give IT and security reviewers direct access to integration, access-control, and handover details.

The first growth audience is operations leaders at businesses that manually read, route, approve, and enter recurring requests across systems. Write for a global English-speaking audience. Sell one scoped custom AI worker project at a time. Use the buyer's terms for the job: request intake automation, approval routing, and CRM or ERP handoffs. Show the worker's role, the context it gets from the Company Brain, and what staff approve.

## Conversion

**Hire AI Workers → Contact page → Project brief → Qualified review → Discovery → Proposal → Signed project**

Use the current Youform through **Open the project brief** on `/contact/`. Ask buyers to describe one recurring task and the tools or rules it depends on. The site owner confirmed that Youform submissions reach them. Enable the email fallback once there is a verified, monitored public address. Qualify an inquiry when it has a recurring workflow, a named owner, feasible system access, and a measurable bottleneck. [Current contact page](https://rsnc.tech/contact/)

The 90-day target is one signed AI worker project from a site inquiry. Count unique qualified inquiries, discoveries held, proposals sent, and projects signed in the existing inquiry inbox or CRM. Link follow-ups to the original inquiry and use the site as the source when intake or the buyer confirms it. Record unknown source or missing access explicitly. Keep contact and project details in the inbox or CRM. Defer the `analytics` skill, site tracking, and conversion rate reporting.

### Buyer sessions

Use `product-marketing` and `customer-research` with existing discovery notes and five sessions with operations or functional leads who own recurring work. Before explaining the offer, show the homepage and operations page. Ask each person to name a task the worker could handle, explain what the Company Brain supplies, and say what happens after clicking **Hire AI Workers**. Then ask which tools the work crosses, who owns exceptions, and what would justify paying for a pilot. Score each session as successful when the buyer can describe the workflow and recognize the CTA as a custom project inquiry. Aim for at least four successes. Keep anonymized buyer wording and objections in the shared product marketing brief.

## Weekly learning loop and decision checkpoints

The maintainer reviews search queries, indexing, the fixed AI search prompts, and anonymized inquiry and discovery themes each week. Identify the most likely bottleneck, select one change to the affected page or intake step, and compare the relevant signal at the next review. Investigate surprising AI answers and wait for repeat evidence before making an editorial change on that basis. When buyer evidence changes who the offer fits or how to describe it, update the shared product marketing brief before writing the next page or proposal.

| Checkpoint | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Day 14 · 7 December 2026 | If fewer than four of five buyers understand the workflow or CTA, revise it before expanding the cluster. Fix crawl or intake failures before promotion. |
| Day 30 · 23 December 2026 | Diagnose indexing gaps for any published priority page. If a page is indexed and gets relevant impressions with few clicks, test a clearer title or opening and compare the next available reporting window. |
| Day 60 · 22 January 2027 | If inquiries are routinely out of scope, clarify who fits and what a suitable workflow looks like. If AI answers repeatedly misstate the offer, improve the source page and rerun the fixed prompts. |
| Day 90 · 21 February 2027 | Build the next page if qualified buyers independently bring up the same adjacent workflow. If evidence is sparse, improve and distribute the existing cluster for another cycle. Revert any change that breaks intake or materially weakens buyer understanding. |

Use one entry per change and update that entry with the outcome at review. If Search Console, Bing, an AI search provider, or the sales inbox is unavailable, record the gap and compare only the evidence collected under matching conditions.
