# Resonance Technology product marketing context

**Version 1.0 — 24 November 2026.** Initial brief based on the website, public portfolio, and approved growth plan. Buyer language and objections below are hypotheses until confirmed in discovery.

## Offer and audience

RSNC builds custom AI workers for recurring business workflows, plus a Company Brain that gives each worker access to approved knowledge, rules, and tools. This is a custom service. The first engagement starts with scoping one request type and agreeing on a measurable pilot. Confirm price, timing, rollout, and operating costs for each project.

The first audience is English-speaking operations leads at businesses with an established request queue and work split across an inbox, internal tools, and a CRM or ERP. The queue owner champions the project, operations leadership owns the business case, and the systems owner reviews access, integration, and handover. A recurring request, named owner, feasible system access, and measurable bottleneck are the four qualification criteria.

## Problem, alternatives, and proof

Start with the manual work: finding the right record, asking for a missing field, preparing approval, and updating a second tool. Compare built-in workflow rules and off-the-shelf intake software for predictable inputs. A custom worker fits when inputs vary, decisions depend on company policy, and the handoff crosses tools.

Explain the review boundary with a specific example: the worker extracts details and proposes the update, then applies it only when policy and approval allow. Show the baseline metric, exception owner, and what happens on a failed write. Likely buyer questions concern system access, human review, time to value, and whether existing tools can do the work.

The public portfolio includes intake and integration work, such as LEARN Behavioral’s ABA intake platform and Wendi’s multi-EHR clinic assistant. Use published scope as related experience. Clear new details or results before quoting them. The request intake walkthrough uses sample data and is labeled illustrative.

## Language and conversion

Use request intake, approval routing, shared inbox, exceptions, CRM handoff, and custom AI worker. Introduce Company Brain by explaining the approved information and rules it supplies. Use concrete tasks, inputs, and measures. **Share one recurring task** leads to the custom project brief at `/contact/`.

Use the [weekly learning loop](../docs/website-refresh-plan.md#weekly-learning-loop-and-decision-checkpoints) for experiments and checkpoint decisions. Update this brief when buyer evidence changes the audience, wording, or offer.
